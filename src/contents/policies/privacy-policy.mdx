# Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how BloodPanda Health Services Pvt. Ltd. and its affiliates ("BloodPanda", "we", "our", or "us") collect, use, share, protect, and otherwise process your information and personal data through:

{/* - <https://www.bloodpanda.com/> */}

- Our mobile applications
- Diagnostic testing services
- Home sample collection services

(collectively, the **Platform**).

You may browse certain sections of the Platform without registration. We do not offer products or services outside India, and your personal data is primarily stored and processed in India.

By accessing the Platform, providing information, or using our services, you agree to this Privacy Policy, our Terms of Use, and any applicable service-specific terms. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Platform.

- Age

- Interests

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Process diagnostic test bookings and orders
- Manage user accounts
- Provide customer support
- Send booking confirmations and reports
- Deliver transactional updates through SMS, email, or WhatsApp
- Personalize user experiences
- Recommend relevant health packages and tests
- Resolve disputes and troubleshoot issues
- Inform users about offers and updates (unless opted out)
- Detect and prevent fraud
- Enforce our Terms of Use and policies
- Conduct analytics, surveys, and research
- Comply with legal obligations

Where required, users may opt out of marketing communications.

---

## 3. Handling of Health Reports

Laboratory reports, prescriptions, and other health-related records ("Health Reports") may be securely digitized and stored.

Subject to applicable law, we may use Health Reports to:

- Conduct internal research
- Improve service quality
- Support external research after anonymization
- Extract report values digitally
- Enable clinical review by qualified professionals
- Generate health trends, alerts, and predictive insights

## 4. Location Information

### Customer Application

With your permission, our customer-facing applications may collect real-time device location information to assist with service delivery.

### Field Collection Application

Our phlebotomist application may collect:

- GPS coordinates
- Latitude and longitude
- Timestamps
- Device identifiers

This information may be collected during active work shifts, including background operation, for purposes such as:

- Route optimization
- Service efficiency
- Verification of sample collection locations
- Verification of sample drop-off locations

You may withdraw location consent by disabling GPS permissions, although this may impact service availability.
