# Diabetes: Eat Smart, Live Better

Diabetes is one of the fastest-growing health conditions worldwide. It occurs when the body cannot effectively regulate blood sugar (glucose) levels. While medications are important, healthy eating is one of the most effective ways to control diabetes and reduce the risk of complications.

## Best Foods for People with Diabetes

### 🥗 High-Fiber Vegetables

Choose plenty of:

- Spinach
- Broccoli
- Cauliflower
- Cabbage
- Bitter gourd (Karela)
- Bottle gourd (Lauki)
- Beans
- Cucumber
- Tomatoes

These vegetables help slow sugar absorption and support healthy digestion.

### 🌾 Whole Grains

Replace refined grains with:

- Brown rice
- Millets (Ragi, Bajra, Jowar)
- Oats
- Whole wheat chapati
- Quinoa

These foods provide steady energy without causing sudden spikes in blood sugar.

### 🍎 Fruits (In Moderation)

Good options include:

- Apple
- Guava
- Pear
- Orange
- Papaya
- Berries

Avoid fruit juices and choose whole fruits whenever possible.

### 🥜 Healthy Protein Sources

Include:

- Eggs
- Fish
- Skinless chicken
- Paneer
- Tofu
- Lentils
- Chickpeas
- Kidney beans (Rajma)

Protein helps keep you full longer and supports blood sugar control.

### 🥛 Low-Fat Dairy

Choose:

- Low-fat milk
- Unsweetened yogurt
- Buttermilk

### 🥜 Healthy Fats

Consume in moderation:

- Almonds
- Walnuts
- Pistachios
- Flaxseeds
- Chia seeds
- Olive oil

## Foods to Limit

Avoid or reduce:

- Sugary drinks
- Sweets and desserts
- White bread
- White rice
- Bakery products
- Processed snacks
- Deep-fried foods

## Lifestyle Tips

- Eat smaller meals every 3–4 hours.
- Walk for at least 30 minutes daily.
- Stay hydrated.
- Monitor your blood sugar regularly.
- Get your HbA1c tested every 3–6 months as advised by your doctor.
