# High Blood Pressure: The Silent Killer

Hypertension often develops without noticeable symptoms, yet it significantly increases the risk of heart attack, stroke, kidney disease, and heart failure. Along with prescribed medications, avoiding certain foods is essential for maintaining healthy blood pressure levels.

## Foods to Avoid

### 🧂 Excess Salt

High sodium intake can raise blood pressure and increase cardiovascular risk.

Avoid:

- Pickles
- Papad
- Chips
- Instant noodles
- Salted snacks
- Processed foods

Aim for less than 5 grams of salt per day (approximately one teaspoon from all sources).

### 🍟 Deep-Fried Foods

Examples include:

- French fries
- Pakoras
- Samosas
- Fried chicken

These foods often contain unhealthy fats that may negatively impact heart health.

### 🍔 Processed and Fast Foods

Limit or avoid:

- Pizza
- Burgers
- Processed meats
- Frozen ready-to-eat meals

These products are commonly high in sodium, calories, and unhealthy fats.

### 🥤 Sugary Drinks

Reduce consumption of:

- Soft drinks
- Energy drinks
- Sweetened fruit juices

These beverages can contribute to obesity and metabolic health issues.

### 🍰 Bakery Products

Limit:

- Cakes
- Cookies
- Pastries
- Doughnuts

These foods often contain excess sugar, salt, and unhealthy fats.

### 🍖 Processed Meats

Examples include:

- Sausages
- Bacon
- Salami
- Ham

Processed meats are among the highest dietary sources of sodium.

## Foods That May Help Support Healthy Blood Pressure

Include more of the following in your diet:

- Bananas
- Spinach
- Beetroot
- Tomatoes
- Oats
- Garlic
- Low-fat dairy products
- Fresh fruits
- Leafy green vegetables

## Healthy Habits for Blood Pressure Control

- Exercise for at least 30 minutes daily.
- Maintain a healthy body weight.
- Avoid smoking.
- Limit alcohol consumption.
- Manage stress effectively.
- Aim for 7–8 hours of quality sleep each night.
